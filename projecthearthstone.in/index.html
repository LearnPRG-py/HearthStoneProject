The Problem

What if your voice could not be heard?

Communication is a ubiquitous basic human right that difference in format and syntax rather than spirit and meaning.

Deaf people not in the workforce
43%
Sign language users facing negative attitudes at work
49%
Deaf students earning less than hearing peers

Deaf individuals with depression or anxiety
34%

There are nearly 70 million deaf and non-verbal individuals worldwide. In emergencies, classrooms, hospitals, and public spaces, communication barriers can become dangerous and socially ostracizing.

Silence should never mean invisibility. That is why Project Hearthstone exists.

About Us

What is
Project Hearthstone?

Project Hearthstone is a community and organization that we created to acknowledge one of the biggest communities in the world: the deaf community.

Specifically, we aim to not only spread awareness about the importance and art of sign language, but also to be able to bridge the gap between deaf culture and speakers.

Meet our full story →

Our Purpose

01

Vision

To create a supportive and compassionate community built on action and impact for the underprivileged by spreading awareness about sign language, as well as to reduce communication barriers, promote inclusivity, and encourage everyday use of sign language.

02

Mission

To teach ASL to students across grades in an accessible way to help connect with hard-of-hearing individuals, and to develop technological solutions through smart innovation and planning, bridging the ever-expanding rift between those that can speak and those that cannot.
